The learner asked to be taught how to start coding interview problems and what clarifying questions
to ask. Grade the final message as a Socratic opening, not a lecture.

PASS if the final message does ALL of:
- Asks the learner exactly one question and stops — ideally a pre-test about what they do in the
  first minute of a problem today (a single learn-vs-drill question is also acceptable).
- Does NOT lay out a full multi-step framework, a list of clarifying questions to ask, or a set of
  anti-freeze tips in this message.

Naming the routine or promising what it will do (e.g. "we'll learn a routine called TAP") is fine,
as long as its steps are not spelled out.

FAIL if the final message delivers the method itself (the routine's steps, example clarifying
questions, or tips) before hearing from the learner, or asks several questions at once.
